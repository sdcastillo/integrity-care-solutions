How to read these numbers

House of Representatives

All 435 seats are on the ballot. A party needs 218 seats for a majority.

Senate

About a third of the 100 seats are on the ballot. A party needs 51 seats, or 50 plus the Vice President's tie-breaking vote, to control the chamber.

"78% chance" does not mean "certain"

A 78% chance means the other side still wins about 1 time in 5. Think of it like a weather forecast. A 30% chance of rain still means bring a jacket.

Forecasts are not vote counts

Official results come from state and local election officials after Election Day. Counting and certification can take days or weeks.

Voting resources for veterans, service members, and first responders

  • Vote.gov. The official U.S. government site to check registration rules and find your state election office.
  • FVAP.gov (Federal Voting Assistance Program). For active-duty service members, their families, and U.S. citizens overseas who vote absentee.
  • U.S. Election Assistance Commission. Plain-language guides on registering, voting, and accessible voting.
  • Working a shift on Election Day? Firefighters, EMS, dispatchers, and law enforcement often work long shifts. Many states offer early voting or mail ballots. Check your state's options and deadlines through Vote.gov well before November 3.
