OUR MISSION

Every person deserves
care delivered with
honesty and dignity.

Integrity Care Resources was founded in 2026 to address a critical gap: military veterans leaving incarceration or under community supervision (probation/parole) face overwhelming barriers to stable employment and housing.

We exist to change that. We provide targeted re-entry navigation, trauma-informed support, career readiness, vocational training, and direct employment placement — with an unwavering commitment to dignity and integrity for every veteran we serve.

WHO WE SERVE

Veterans and the people who run toward the call.

Integrity Care Resources was built first for justice-involved and recently released military veterans. We also walk alongside firefighters, EMS, law enforcement, dispatchers, and other emergency services professionals who carry trauma, employment gaps, or re-entry barriers after service to their communities.

Military veterans

Justice-involved and recently released veterans, transitioning service members, and military families.

Firefighters

Career and volunteer firefighters seeking stable civilian careers, credentials, and trauma-informed support.

EMS & Paramedics

Emergency medical technicians, paramedics, and first responders rebuilding after burnout, injury, or career transition.

Law enforcement

Officers and deputies navigating retirement, injury, or re-entry into civilian life with dignity and purpose.

Dispatchers & 911

Public safety telecommunicators carrying secondary trauma from high-stress call environments.

All emergency services

Anyone who served in uniform — military, fire, EMS, law enforcement, dispatch — and needs a stable path forward.

OUR PROGRAMS

Pathways to stability and purpose.

Four integrated programs designed to meet people where they are and move them toward sustainable employment.

V-REP

Veterans Re-Entry Employment Pathway — 16 to 20 week cohort for justice-involved veterans within 6-18 months of release.

Learn more

Fast-Track

Employability Skills Fast-Track — 2 to 4 week intensive building workplace competencies for any industry.

Learn more

Logistics

Industrial Logistics & Transportation — warehouse, forklift, CDL, and supply chain pathways.
