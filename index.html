OUR MISSION

Every person deserves care delivered with honesty and dignity.

Integrity Care Resources was founded in 2026 to address a critical gap: military veterans leaving incarceration or under community supervision face overwhelming barriers to stable employment and housing.

We provide targeted re-entry navigation, trauma-informed support, career readiness, vocational training, and direct employment placement — with an unwavering commitment to dignity and integrity.

WHO WE SERVE

Veterans and the people who run toward the call.

Built first for justice-involved and recently released military veterans. We also walk alongside firefighters, EMS, law enforcement, dispatchers, and other emergency services professionals.

Military veterans

Justice-involved and recently released veterans, transitioning service members, and military families.

Firefighters

Career and volunteer firefighters navigating trauma, employment transitions, or community reintegration.

EMS

Emergency medical technicians and paramedics seeking stable employment and peer support.

Law enforcement

Officers and deputies facing employment gaps, trauma, or re-entry barriers after service.

Dispatchers

911 and emergency dispatch professionals seeking career pathways and support.

Other first responders

Emergency services professionals carrying the weight of community service and seeking a path forward.
