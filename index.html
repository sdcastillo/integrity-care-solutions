VETERANS FIRST

Veterans: The Backbone of Our Operations

Veterans will serve as the workforce and backbone of our operations.

Their military experience positions them strongly for meaningful employment in the reentry field.

It’s a true win-win for everyone.

We will actively recruit veterans through the VA to fill key positions across our programs.

Unemployed or underemployed individuals can also enroll in our fast-paced training programs.

Our Mission

We equip individuals — including those facing reentry barriers — with in-demand trade skills, certifications, and the support they need to secure stable employment and build brighter futures. We work in partnership with employers, training providers, and community organizations across Western Massachusetts.

Core Life & Employment Skills

These foundational skills are non-negotiable for long-term success in any career path.

  • • Resume building & interview preparation
  • • Digital communication & workplace tools
  • • Time management, reliability & professionalism
  • • Workplace communication & conflict resolution
  • • Financial literacy & understanding workplace rights

Entrepreneurship & Self-Employment Track

Especially valuable for individuals facing traditional hiring barriers.

  • • Starting small service businesses (landscaping, cleaning, auto detailing)
  • • Marketing basics & customer acquisition
  • • Financial literacy, budgeting, taxes & credit repair
