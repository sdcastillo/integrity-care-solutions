CAREER DEVELOPMENT TRAINING PROGRAM

Purpose-Built for Veterans Within 6-18 Months of Release

V-REP prepares military veterans within 6-18 months of release or currently on probation or parole for sustainable employment. The program is specifically designed to overcome the barriers these veterans face: gaps in employment, PTSD and trauma history, limited digital literacy, home instability or homelessness, and lack of certifications or recent job skills.

Our Mission

Remove barriers to meaningful employment for military veterans within 6-18 months of release or on probation/parole. We equip them with the skills, certifications, digital literacy, and trauma support needed to succeed in American manufacturing and skilled trades.

Made in America Alignment

Every participant is trained for roles that strengthen domestic supply chains, support reshoring efforts, and contribute to national economic and defense resilience.

Trauma-Informed Approach

We recognize the compounded impact of military service and incarceration. All programming is delivered with military cultural competency, PTSD awareness, and deep respect for the trauma history many participants carry.

EXPANDING CAREER PIPELINES

The Veterans Workforce Development and Training Incubator (VWDTI)

The Veterans Workforce Development and Training Incubator (VWDTI) will leverage Integrity Care Resources' commitment to integrity, service, and community impact by creating career pipelines in healthcare, behavioral health, home care, social services, information technology, logistics, and small business development. The initiative aligns with national workforce development priorities and leading career wisdom.

MISSION

To equip veterans and their families with specialized training and strategic career placements that accelerate professional development and maximize their potential for executive leadership and advancement to the highest levels of the career ladder.

VISION

To become the premier veteran-focused workforce development hub that transforms battle-tested military experience into civilian career success while strengthening communities through skilled, purpose-driven leadership.

1. Increase veteran employment and long-term job retention.

3. Develop leadership and entrepreneurship abilities.
4. Connect veterans to form networks of colleagues and professional connections in new fields.
5. Support veteran-owned startups and turn brilliant ideas into profitable business ventures.

7. Improve economic outcomes for military families, spouses, and children.

Sustainability Plan

The incubator will create long-term sustainability through diversified revenue streams and mission-aligned business models, including:

1. Workforce training contracts with government agencies and employers.
2. Employer-sponsored training programs tailored to specific industry needs.
3. Apprenticeship funding through registered programs and government incentives.
4. Grant funding diversification across federal, state, private foundation, and corporate sources.
5. Fee-for-service workforce solutions, including customized training and placement services.
6. Corporate partnerships that provide both funding and direct hiring pipelines for veterans.
7. Social enterprise initiatives (detailed below).
Social Enterprise Initiatives

Social enterprise initiatives are mission-driven businesses operated by the incubator that generate sustainable revenue while directly advancing veteran employment and community impact. Unlike traditional nonprofits that rely primarily on donations and grants, social enterprises use market-based strategies to create earned income streams.

In the context of VWDTI, this means launching and scaling veteran-led or veteran-supporting ventures such as:

  • A veteran-owned staffing and recruitment agency that places program graduates into roles while reinvesting profits back into training scholarships.
  • Specialized consulting and training services (e.g., military-to-civilian transition workshops sold to corporations) where revenue funds incubator operations.
  • Impact-focused small business ventures in logistics, home care, or IT services that employ veterans and generate ongoing income for the program.

Profits from these enterprises are reinvested to reduce reliance on external funding, create real-world employment opportunities during training, and ensure the incubator can continue serving justice-involved veterans long-term regardless of fluctuations in grant cycles.

YOUR PATH FORWARD

The V-REP Participant Journey

1
Orientation & Barrier Assessment (Week 1–2)

Intake, employment gap analysis, trauma-informed assessment, digital literacy screening, housing status review, individualized career plan, and connection to peer coach and case manager.

2
Career Readiness Intensive (Week 3–5)

Professional skills, financial wellness, interview training, and credential preparation (OSHA, forklift, etc.).

3
Vocational Training Track (Week 6–14)

Hands-on training at partner facilities or our training center. Earn industry-recognized certifications.

4
Job Placement & Transition (Week 15–18)

Employer interviews, job offers, and onboarding support. Family transition planning.

5
12-Month Retention Support

Ongoing coaching, peer check-ins, crisis support, and advancement planning to ensure long-term success.
