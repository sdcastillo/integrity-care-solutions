--- permalink: /blog/first-responders-shared-paths.html --- # First Responders and Veterans: Shared Paths to Stability Firefighters, EMS, law enforcement, dispatchers, and military veterans carry different uniforms, but the transition challenges often look the same: trauma, employment gaps, housing instability, and the need for trauma-informed support. Integrity Care Resources was built first for justice-involved veterans. We now extend the same re-entry navigation, career readiness, and vocational training to emergency services professionals who need a second chance or a steadier path forward. ## What We Offer - Re-entry navigation and community supervision support - Trauma-informed peer support - Hands-on trade skills training (welding, logistics, construction) - Direct employment placement - Housing and benefits navigation If you or someone you know served in uniform — military or emergency services — we are here to help.
