--- permalink: /blog/why-trade-skills-matter.html --- # Why Trade Skills Matter for Reentry Certifications in welding, logistics, and construction open doors that traditional resumes often can't. For someone rebuilding after incarceration or a difficult transition out of emergency services, a recognized trade credential is proof of reliability and skill. It is a concrete step toward stable employment and housing. Our programs focus on employer-recognized training, not just theory. Participants leave with the tools and the confidence to start working.
