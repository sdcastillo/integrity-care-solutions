WHY LOGISTICS & TRANSPORTATION

Stable Work. Clear Advancement. Real Demand.

Warehousing, distribution, and freight move the American economy every day. Employers need reliable people who can follow procedures, work as a team, and keep goods flowing safely — strengths that align closely with military and structured-work experience.

Hiring at Scale

Distribution centers, third-party logistics (3PL) providers, manufacturers, and carriers regularly hire entry-level and mid-skill workers with short certification pathways.

Room to Grow

Start as a warehouse associate or material handler and advance into lead, inventory control, dispatcher, supervisor, or commercial driver roles.

Second-Chance Friendly

Many logistics and industrial employers are open to fair-chance hiring. We help participants prepare, certify, and present their strengths with confidence.

Hands-on skills that transfer directly to warehouses, yards, and industrial facilities.

TRAINING TRACKS

What You Will Learn

Choose a focused pathway — or combine modules — based on your goals, driving eligibility, and local employer demand.

Warehouse & Material Handling

Core skills for distribution centers, fulfillment, and manufacturing logistics floors.

  • Receiving & shipping operations — inbound checks, staging, outbound packing and labeling
  • Inventory fundamentals — cycle counts, SKU accuracy, location systems
  • Pick, pack & put-away — RF scanners, barcodes, and workflow efficiency
  • Safety culture — PPE, hazard awareness, and warehouse best practices

Forklift & Powered Industrial Trucks

Certification-focused training that unlocks higher-wage floor roles.

  • Sit-down & stand-up forklift operation — load stability, stacking, and traffic rules
  • Pre-use inspections — OSHA-aligned daily checks and documentation
  • Pallet jack & order picker basics — common warehouse equipment family
  • Workplace evaluation readiness — prepare for employer-site operator sign-off

Supply Chain & Logistics Coordination

Entry-level roles that can grow into supervisory and planning positions.

  • Order fulfillment workflows — from customer order to dock door
  • Basic logistics software — WMS/TMS concepts, spreadsheets, digital documentation
  • Vendor & carrier communication — professional updates, problem escalation
  • Lead & team coordination — shift handoffs, metrics, and continuous improvement

Transportation & Commercial Driving Prep

Pathways into local delivery, yard operations, and CDL careers.

  • CDL Class A/B readiness support — study guidance, partner school referrals, and career mapping
  • Hours of service & DOT basics — compliance awareness for professional drivers
  • Local delivery & last-mile roles — route planning, customer service, vehicle checks
  • Yard hostler / switcher intro — trailer spotting and terminal operations concepts
CREDENTIALS THAT OPEN DOORS

Certifications & Skills Employers Recognize

General industry safety foundations valued across warehouses and industrial sites.

Forklift / Powered Industrial Truck Operator

CDL Prep Pathway

Email, spreadsheets, scanners, and basic warehouse systems used on modern docks and floors.
