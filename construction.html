Building Our Future:
A New Home for Veterans in Southampton

We are constructing a state-of-the-art facility right here in town to expand housing, training, and comprehensive support services for veterans and their families.

Support the Project View the Budget
Total Project Cost: $48 Million  |  Initial Funding Ask: $15 Million
OUR VISION

A Permanent Home for Hope, Healing, and Opportunity

This new facility will serve as the cornerstone of our mission in Southampton, Massachusetts. Built locally by our community, it will provide integrated housing, intensive training programs, and wraparound support services under one roof.

By constructing here in town, we are not only creating a lasting resource for veterans but also generating local jobs, supporting the economy, and strengthening our community for generations to come.

What the Facility Will Include

  • Long-term supportive housing units
  • State-of-the-art training classrooms and workshops
  • On-site mental health and wellness services
  • Job placement and career development center
  • Community spaces for veterans and families
  • Administrative and program operations headquarters

TEAM & OPERATIONS

Staffing Organization

The new facility will be operated by a highly qualified, veteran-centered team dedicated to delivering integrated housing, training, and support services.

Program Director (Veteran Services Lead)

Oversees all program operations, strategic planning, and service delivery. Ensures alignment with veteran-centered values and coordinates across housing, training, and support teams.

Career Development Coaches

Provide one-on-one guidance on career planning, resume building, interview skills, and professional development to help veterans achieve sustainable employment.

Licensed Behavioral Health Counselor (Trauma-Informed)

Delivers trauma-informed mental health support, counseling, and crisis intervention tailored to the unique experiences of justice-involved and transitioning veterans.

Employment Placement Specialist (Key Role)

Builds and maintains employer partnerships, matches veterans to job opportunities, and provides ongoing job retention support to ensure long-term success.

Peer Veteran Mentors

Lived-experience mentors who provide peer support, navigation assistance, and role modeling to help participants build confidence and community.

Administrative & Case Management Support

Handles intake, case coordination, benefits navigation, scheduling, and administrative functions to ensure seamless service delivery and accurate record-keeping.

This multidisciplinary team will work collaboratively to deliver holistic, veteran-centered services in the new facility.
